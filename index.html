⚙ Tools

Quick Guide:

  • 1. Select Folder: Choose the main directory containing your AIRAC cycles.
  • 2. Filter: Type at least 3 letters in Filter Country to search. You can also search by Cells and Chart Types.
  • 3. Navigate: Click on any item in the list to open the chart.
  • 4. PDF Search: Use Ctrl + F while the PDF is focused to find text inside the chart.
  • 5. Cycles: Use the dropdown at the top-right to switch between AIRACs.
